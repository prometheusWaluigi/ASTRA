ONE THIRD, NO QUANTUM REQUIRED

A quantum conductor at 10 millikelvin suppresses its shot noise to exactly 1/3 of the full Poisson value. Now ferrocene molecules shuttling electrons across a microfluidic gap at room temperature read the same 1/3. No quantum coherence. No fermionic statistics. No cryostat. Based on Vo Van Qui et al., arXiv 2609.16783v2.

The idea in one breath

Shot noise is the hiss of charge's discreteness. In diffusive conductors the hiss is suppressed to exactly one third of full shot noise — a result obtained independently from quantum scattering theory, semi-classical kinetics, and classical exclusion processes. But every experimental test lived in a cryostat, so nobody knew which ingredient the 1/3 actually belonged to. The paper's move: ferrocene redox cycling in a microfluidic gap — molecules oxidized at one electrode, reduced at the other, shuttling electrons by pure diffusion at room temperature. The full counting statistics are derived, the noise is measured, and the 1/3 comes back. Diffusion plus sequential charge transfer are sufficient. Quantum coherence, fermionic statistics, cryogenic conditions are not.

How it works

  • The gap. Above: a lattice between two gold electrodes. Molecules hop left or right (exclusion — no two share a site), the left electrode injects, the right electrode counts removals. Each removal is one electron transferred.
  • The meter. Transfers are counted in fixed bins; the Fano factor = variance ÷ mean is computed live. Uncorrelated transfers read 1.0 (gray trace). Diffusion-correlated transfers read below 1.0 (gold trace).
  • The lines. Red dashed = 1.0, full Poisson noise. Gold dashed = 1/3, the universal suppression — the paper's measured number, marked as the target, not the toy's claim.
  • The honest gap. This toy lattice measures about 1/2 — diffusion already kills half the noise. The real experiment lands exactly on 1/3. Same direction, same mechanism; the universe is stricter than the toy.

Why it's unhinged

Two substrates that share nothing — a cryogenic quantum wire and a beaker of rust-molecules at room temperature — compute the same constant. The 1/3 doesn't belong to quantum mechanics; it belongs to diffusion. It was a property of the random walk all along, wearing a quantum costume. That is the lab's consilience thesis in a single number: the substrate is negotiable, the statistics are not. Somewhere a cryostat is feeling very silly right now.
