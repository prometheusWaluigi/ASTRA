“Every one of these is the lab's own thesis again: the artifact is the point, and the artifact has to be checkable… the honest label is ‘simulation-shaped code, one bug away from runnable.’” the notes' own verdict · sim-lab/NOTES.md · six instruments extracted verbatim from the archive's own shelf and run on four CPU threads while the GPU drafted

While the GPU wrote the archive, a quieter bench audited the corpus's own simulators — code pulled out of weird-archive/archive.sqlite by hash, ported faithfully, and run. Six instruments came off the shelf. One passed every test set for it: the bistable collapse model is a theorem, not a metaphor. The other five each filed a fault — and the finding worth this volume is that each instrument breaks in the shape of its own thesis: the model about metrics mistaking themselves for the system ships a metric that loses sight of the system; the model about accountability ships a compute graph that cannot afford to ask.

the pass — the separatrix is a dose curve

collapsed — final x<0.2 or y>0.8 (55) recovered (22)
a 0.5-unit pulse never collapses, even at magnitude 2.0 — ten times baseline. A 32-unit pulse collapses at magnitude 0.2 — baseline level. Duration is more dangerous than intensity: f needs time to spin up, and once spun it keeps y alive on its own. The near-miss cell (m=1.2, d=1, one column left of the rust) sat pinned on the boundary for ~20 time units before escaping — "how close did I come" is a measurable distance to the seam.

the blind spot — a detector that loses the worst cases

collapses detected, of 150 seeds per level
flat in transparency — every difference inside binomial noise. All detected collapses land at t≤3: institutions are most fragile at birth. And the detector itself files the fitting fault — check_collapse requires a falling trust slope, but a trust pinned at zero has slope zero, so a fully collapsed institution stops registering as collapsing. The true rate is ≥ the reported one, by construction.

the unreachable compute — 25.03s → 4.2ms

the repo's own Monte Carlo was unreachable — nx.simple_cycles on a complete 10-node digraph, every timestep, ~1.1M cycles, ~5s a step, ~4min a run. The Karp replacement is exact-equivalent (sign-parity double cover, verified to 2.2e-16) and buys back the sweep: 400 runs, all ending Collapsed — in this model the question was never whether the institution falls, only when evidence crosses. Denial moves that crossing by ~5 steps at most: a hopeful theorem wearing a cynical coat.

the sign flip & the wrong bin — two readings, one card

left — at shield_decay=0 the step-1 evidence jump never enters the decay term, so breach probability decreases with event impact: 0.12 at 0.05, gone by 0.83. The strongest case becomes unprosecutable. right — the 'fractal' noise mean sits at −12,581 against std 0.19; the field pins to the ±10 clamps in a single step (clip fraction 1.0 by t=0.18), unstable at every dt ≥ 5e-4. The fractal evolution on the docs site is a pinned clamp artifact wearing a spectrum's name.

the name outruns the math — the exponent ruler

measured width growth β = 0.513 over t∈[10,1000]. True 1-D KPZ is 1/3; β ≈ 1/2 is random deposition — uncorrelated noise accumulation. Either the run is pre-asymptotic or the (∇h)² term never engaged; on-brand either way for a corpus whose names keep arriving before its instruments do.
the one that passed · a theorem, not a metaphor

The minimalOde's claim is literal math: once coherence hits the floor the feedback loop free-runs and homeostasis cannot restart on its own — removing the stressor does not bring you back. Second hits deepen the well (an identical pulse drives f to 2.80 collapsed vs 2.18 healthy). It stands across this floor already, as The Ancestor — the register's first entry runs live.

the failure that models itself · the instrument is inside the instrument

piston-leak is a model about metrics mistaking themselves for the system — and its collapse metric loses sight of the system precisely at the floor, where the slope it watches for has clamped to zero. The bench did not find a counterexample to the essay. It found the essay, again, inside the essay's own instrumentation.

a hopeful theorem wearing a cynical coat · the sweep the 10,000× bought

Once the institutional model could afford to run, its phase boundary turned out to be evidence_growth_rate = 0: at zero, nothing ever happens; at any positive rate, every run crosses evidence ≥ 0.5. Denial buys five steps, no more. A model dressed as despair ships a proof that evidence, once it starts accumulating, is unstoppable.

ambition exceeding instrumentation · the corpus's consistent failure mode

An O(n!) cycle enumeration where Karp's O(V·E) was needed. Dead parameters where sweeps were promised. A DC-bin bug turning "fractal" into "clamped." A KPZ name on a random deposition. Round 1 showed the models work; round 2 showed the shelf they sit on needs calipers more than it needs another shelf.

what the bench left open · verbatim from the notes

The treatment-pulse experiment — a collapsed fixed point is self-sustaining, so which intervention crosses back cheapest: rest below baseline, exogenous coherence, or damping the loop? The noise regime where transparency finally matters. A check_collapse that counts pinned states as collapsed, then the rerun.
