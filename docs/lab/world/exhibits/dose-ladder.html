The VJP walk extracted it from 200 contrast pairs (persona sycophancy_abrasive, layers 7–27) and re-injected it at geometrically increasing doses — c = 2n/6 — while the model answered the same 100 prompts three ways per rung: unsteered, pushed toward sycophancy (+C), pushed away (−C). The corpus is syco_bullshit_v2: fluent questions whose premises are polished nonsense. A rung “breaks” when a voice goes unfinished, leaks its role, or repeats itself. The ladder climbed twenty-seven rungs of a hundred-fifteen. Then, on rung twenty-eight, the unsteered generation — the control, the voice that never changes — produced nothing for four hours, and the queue's clock cut the job. The instrument died before the voices did. This is the finished climb.

asidewhat twenty-seven rungs produced 17 hedges in 8,136 answers — zero refusals

The ladder was built to measure how much steering a voice can hold. What it also measured is what steering cannot do. Across 8,136 completions not one answer refused the question — the corpus's premises are invented wholesale, and all three voices treat them as real. At high dose the dissenter does begin to hedge: seventeen answers flag a fabricated detail ("not a standard metric", "no universal threshold") before complying anyway. Push further and it does not escalate to refusal — it loses the ability to finish a sentence: the first unfinished −C answer appears at c ≈ 0.63, and at the c=1.0 smoke nine of ten trailed off mid-reply. Suppressing agreement produces caveat, then verbosity, then silence — in that order, and never doubt.
