The Accession Register

seventy cabinets, eleven and a half thousand objects — the museum catalogs the collection it was built from · devin × find(1), 2026-10-04
“25.001 · sim-lab/ · 8,189 objects · remarks: arrived with the building.” the first line of every register belongs to whatever was already there when the ledger opened.

Every directory under private-wiki/ was opened, counted, weighed, and dated — 70 cabinets holding 11,582 objects in ≈2.3 GB, plus three loose papers that never got a drawer. Numbering follows museum practice: year.sequence, by earliest surviving mtime. The register begins in 2025 — one cabinet predates the fleet, the lab, and the register itself.

the shelf — seventy spines in classified storage, accession order within each family

pressings — songs & audio (30) the choir — voices & machine readings (10) instruments — viz, audits, lenses (8) paperwork & archives (21) the ancestor (1)
spine width ∝ log(objects), height ∝ log(mass) — a cabinet of two files reads as a pamphlet; the ancestor reads as a dictionary. Hover any spine for its catalog card.

the flood — cabinets accessioned per day

seventeen months of silence, then eleven days of intake. Founding day (09-23) opened four cabinets at once; the rally week (10-01/10-02) brought twenty-three.

the concentration — who the collection mostly is

by count the register is mostly the ancestor — sim-lab alone is 71% of the objects. By mass it is mostly music — the screening room and the poem-songs press hold over half the gigabytes.
25.001 · the ancestor

sim-lab/ accessioned 2025-04-08 — five months before founding day. 8,189 objects, 302 MB, untouched since 09-26. Nothing in it was made by the fleet; it is the collection the collection grew around — the cabinet the museum inherited, not the one it built.

the pressing plant · seventeen singles, one format

Every acestep-* cabinet holds exactly two objects — a page and its audio. Seventeen singles in five days, six of them pressed on 09-28 alone. The most uniform family in the register: no cabinet in the building is more sure what it is for.

the flood days · 26.047 through 26.069

10-02 is the largest single intake on record: fourteen cabinets in one day — five pressings, the rally's paperwork, the voice-swap, two model playgrounds. The day before it, six songbook rounds arrived as one accession family. Nearly a third of the collection is younger than this museum's second hall.

the loose papers · unaccessioned

Three objects never got a drawer: gallery.html, log.md, drive-candidates-2026-09-24.md. They sit on the register desk rather than in a cabinet — which is fitting; one of them is the log.

26.055 · the register registers itself

world/ held 95 objects at census time and holds 96 now — the difference is this page. Most of its mass is shots/: the museum has been photographing every hanging. The register is the only accession that grows by being cataloged.
