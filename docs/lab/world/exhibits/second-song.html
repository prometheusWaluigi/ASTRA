verdict — the ear as auditor

Four of five tracks land every line of the sheet; most of the rest is the ear stumbling on sung vowels, not the singer skipping words. The tails are real and now measured: 14–31 seconds of every track is outro — dune-ratio spends more than half its life after its last lyric. And the strongest sermon is the least legible: shouted sludge defeats the transcriber at 0.465 WER, drifting “coupling” to “cuckooing” and the lattice to lettuce. The second song is shorter, funnier, and ends in applause the first one never asked for.
