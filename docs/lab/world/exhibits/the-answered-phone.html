The Oracle of Chemnitz: an interactive art installation to reanimate old things in a garage featuring a rotary phone
Old Eastern Bloc garages became temporary ateliers. The installation gave a typewriter, a radio, a desk, tires, a mixer and a rotary-dial telephone each a name, a personality and a story. The phone rings when a visitor approaches; answered, it asks your name and month of birth, tells a device’s story, and hints at other places in the city. ~2,700 visitors picked up over three months.
