🏛 the museum of corrections

an annex of the lab gallery — three art-ouroboros chains (VL plaque → persona rewrite → ComfyUI render → VL correction), six frames, and placards that argue with each other. Sibling of the Unreliable Narrators wing.

epistemic signage: persona names inside the chains are simulated Qwen voices — no agent wrote those lines. Jaccard values are lexical similarity (word overlap between neighboring texts), never a semantic-drift score. Fictional researcher names in the chain texts remain unverified fiction. Most corrections end mid-sentence — flagged per frame.

how to visit: each frame hangs four layers side by side — claim, rewrite, render, correction. Mark per layer what is visible in the image, what is only asserted, where the readings conflict. Vote the contested placards. ←→ move between frames, E exports your marks as JSON, X resets. All controls are radios/textareas — tab-focusable, state lives in this browser's localStorage only. Nothing leaves the file.
