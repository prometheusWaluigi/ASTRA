🏛 the unreliable poetry museum

a reveal-and-compare exhibit · pieces · local-only, nothing leaves this page

You will be shown short pieces of writing. For each one:

  1. Respond first. Write a line or two about its texture — what it felt like, what it did to your attention. Then guess who made it.
  2. Then lift the placard. The reveal shows the true attribution, the source fragment it grew from, and the seams where it was joined.

This is a museum, not a study. Your guesses are kept in this browser only; nothing is collected, scored, or sent anywhere. Being wrong is the point of the room.
