The weave

Twenty-four warp threads in gold, eighteen weft in dim mercury — each weft bulges alternately over and under the warp it crosses, so the interlace is structural, not painted. The whole cloth breathes on a slow phase. Papers sit at crossings as knots: points where two directions of constraint meet and something survives the tension.
