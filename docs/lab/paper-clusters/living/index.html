The grammar

Each regeneration runs the same four local rules from Minimal branching and fusion morphogenesis: a hyphal tip elongates with a wandering bias, branches on a slow clock, fuses when it meets an older strand — the seam becomes a loop — and stops as its energy runs out. No global optimizer; the Pareto front of transport, exploration, and robustness arrives anyway. Papers hang where strands end or fuse — fruiting bodies, not bullets.
