The strand

A (p,q) torus knot winds p times around the torus's axis and q times through the hole — two coprime integers deciding everything. Beads sit at equal arc-length intervals, so the papers are literally syntax: an order on a strand that cannot be re-sequenced without breaking it. The mercury companion knot threads the same crossings a half-turn out of phase — pairwise linking, made visible. Each REKNOT turns the (p,q) dial: trefoil (2,3), cinquefoil (2,5), and beyond.
