Field note i — what the expedition brought home

The openai/math repo is 722 AI-produced manuscripts sorted into 372 families across 17 disciplines. Most of it is not results — it is claims, and claims are the raw material this desk exists for. The antipattern field tests found “formalization theater” in the headline families: proofs that gesture at rigor without carrying it. So the three instruments here were built to keep the claim and the checking in one frame — every number labeled claimed-vs-established, every wall drawn where the argument says it stands.

Field note ii — three ways of checking

The Zero-Free Frontier takes the headline claim — a critical strip free of zeros for Re(s) > 11/12 — and draws the strip itself, the claimed wall marked as claimed. Mahler’s Mirror makes the volume-product conjecture tactile: drag the polygon, watch the polar body and the area-product gauge move live, and feel where the inequality bites. The Verification Map is the whole expedition at once — all 372 families as tiles, checked vs unchecked, the eight headliners ringed in gold.

Field note iii — the desk does not grade

None of these instruments declares the expedition good or bad. They mount it the way the field station mounts papers: the claim is the specimen, the checking is the room it sits in. Where a page says “claimed,” read it as the desk being honest about which side of the glass the evidence stands on. The repo’s own framing is preserved verbatim — formalization theater included, because naming the theater is also a kind of checking.
