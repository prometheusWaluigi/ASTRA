What the trilogy does not say

Residuals are descriptive, not p-values. No holdout was registered. Constants, targets and grammar were chosen after the match was known, so conditional coverage is not a discovery statistic. A relation could still be a boundary condition at a scale outside the tested window — running away from a claim does not exclude it holding elsewhere.

Section C running is one-loop, yt-only, on 2014 matched inputs; a current-edition pass is reported separately and never mixed. Identities like φ⁸+1=7φ⁴ follow from φ²=φ+1 and carry no physics.
